The History of Formula 1

From its early beginnings to the global spectacle it is today

The First Steps (1950)

Formula 1 was officially born in 1950, but its roots go back to Grand Prix racing in the 1920s and 1930s. The first World Championship was held at Silverstone, laying the foundation for what would become the greatest show in motorsport.

The Golden Era (1960–1980)

During these two decades, F1 experienced pure magic. Drivers like Jim Clark, Jackie Stewart, and Niki Lauda defined what it meant to be a world champion, while technology evolved rapidly.

Technological Revolution (1980–2000)

The introduction of turbo engines, advanced electronic systems, and composite materials completely transformed the sport. This era saw the rise of legends like Ayrton Senna and Michael Schumacher.

Modern Era (2000–Present)

With the arrival of hybrid engines, DRS systems, and advanced telemetry, modern F1 blends tradition with innovation, remaining at the forefront of global automotive development.
